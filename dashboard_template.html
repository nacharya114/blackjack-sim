A spread is a 1-to-N bet ramp: 1 unit at true count ≤+1, ramping to N units at +5. Where house edge crosses zero is the breakeven spread; the smallest spread at or past it is the lowest-risk one (least volatility). See docs/bet_spreads.md.

Cells are the engine's basic strategy; drag the true count slider to see the Hi-Lo deviations (outlined, brass) the counter switches to at that count. Only hard totals deviate (the Illustrious 18 are hard hands + insurance). With Show action EVs on, each cell is tinted by how marginal the decision is (warmer = closer call) and clicking a cell shows every action's EV at the selected count. EVs are exact for hard & soft totals; pair rows show the hand played as its total (the split action's EV is not modelled yet). See docs/strategy_deviations.md.
